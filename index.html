Built on Steel,
Founded on Trust

Sri Balamurugan Steel Furniture was founded in 1996 by V. Devaraj with a simple mission: to bring workshop-quality steel furniture directly to Tamil Nadu homes at honest prices.

Starting with a small fabrication unit in Gudiyatham, we've grown into a trusted manufacturer serving over 10k families across all 10+ districts. Every piece is designed, fabricated, and quality-checked in our own workshop.

We believe furniture should be built to last — that's why we use only premium CRCA steel with anti-rust powder coating, guaranteed to stay strong for decades.

1996 — Founded in Gudiyatham

Started with almirahs and home furniture from a small workshop

2010 — Expanded to Office Furniture

Added chairs, workstations, and storage systems

2016 — All-TN Delivery Network

Logistics now covering all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu

2026 — Online Custom Configurator

Digital platform launched for bespoke furniture orders
